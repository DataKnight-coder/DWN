## Safety checks before you share anything

- A real platform never charges you to apply, test, train or get paid. If anyone asks for money, stop.
- Start only from the platform's official website. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a message.
- Be careful with unsolicited messages on WhatsApp, Telegram or social media, even if they use a real company's name.
- Only upload identity documents inside the official platform, and never send them by chat or email to a "recruiter".
- Read [how to spot AI training and remote job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/) before you apply, and follow the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's guidance if something feels wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Mindrift legit?

I cannot vouch for any company. Use the official pages, read independent experiences, never pay to join, and judge a platform by whether its terms are clear and its payments are documented.

### Is it free to apply?

Yes. A legitimate platform does not charge applicants. If you are asked to pay, treat it as a scam.

### Is the work guaranteed once I am accepted?

No. Projects open and close, and being accepted does not promise a steady supply of tasks. Plan for uneven work.
