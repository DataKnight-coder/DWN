# Mindrift Guide: Freelance AI Trainer Work Explained

Mindrift is a freelance platform where experts help train AI models. It is operated by Toloka AI. You may see it searched as "Mindrift" or misspelled, and people usually ask how it works and whether it is legitimate. This guide relies mainly on Mindrift's own pages, which are the company's descriptions and not independent reviews.

## How to apply, step by step

1. Read the explanation on Mindrift's official site.
2. Apply with your CV and background. Mindrift says it reviews applications and contacts people who fit.
3. Pass the qualification assessments for the project area you want.
4. Join a project when one opens, read the guidelines and follow them closely.
5. Complete tasks, check the pay shown before each one, and keep your own records.

## What the screening checks

Qualifications test your subject knowledge and writing. Typical listings ask for strong written English, relevant education or experience and a stable internet connection. Specialist projects may ask for more. The guide to [preparing for an AI training interview](/blog/ai-interview-assessment-prep/) explains the common formats.

## Pay and payment

Mindrift's own site lists a wide pay range that depends on project complexity and your expertise, and says compensation is shown before you begin each task. Individual postings show different rates. Mindrift also says projects are never guaranteed, and a period without a project does not mean you have been removed. Treat the work as flexible and uneven.

## Things to keep in mind

- Mindrift's blog describes itself as a source, so also read independent experiences before you decide.
- There is no employment contract and no fixed hours, so you manage your own schedule and taxes.
- Available work changes over time. No guide can promise tasks, income or acceptance.

## Who Mindrift may suit

People with solid writing and a field of expertise who want flexible, project-by-project work. Compare other options in [AI training platforms compared](/blog/ai-training-platforms-compared/) and read the full [AI training jobs guide](/blog/ai-training-jobs-guide/).

## Safety checks before you share anything

- A real platform never charges you to apply, test, train or get paid. If anyone asks for money, stop.
- Start only from the platform's official website. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a message.
- Be careful with unsolicited messages on WhatsApp, Telegram or social media, even if they use a real company's name.
- Only upload identity documents inside the official platform, and never send them by chat or email to a "recruiter".
- Read [how to spot AI training and remote job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/) before you apply, and follow the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's guidance if something feels wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Mindrift legit?

I cannot vouch for any company. Use the official pages above, read independent experiences, never pay to join, and judge the platform by whether its terms are clear and its payments are documented.

### Is it free to apply?

Yes. A legitimate platform does not charge applicants. If you are asked to pay, treat it as a scam.

### Is the work guaranteed once I am accepted?

No. Projects open and close, and being accepted does not promise a steady supply of tasks. Plan for uneven work.

### Do I need to pay tax on this income?

In Canada, income from contract work is generally taxable and you may need to report it yourself. Ask the Canada Revenue Agency or a tax professional about your situation.

### Can I do Mindrift work alongside another job?

Often yes, because the work is flexible and project based. Check each project's time expectations first.

**Educational note:** This guide is general information, not an endorsement of any company, and cannot promise acceptance or income. Platforms change their rules often, so confirm details on their official sites. Results vary.
