# Foreign Credential Recognition in Alberta: Using IQAS

If you studied outside Canada, an employer, school or licensing body may ask for proof of how your education compares to Canadian standards. In Alberta, the main government service for that is the International Qualifications Assessment Service (IQAS). This guide explains what IQAS does, what it does not do, and how it fits with licensing and immigration steps.

## What IQAS is

IQAS is an Alberta government service. It compares educational credentials earned outside Canada to educational standards in Canada and issues an assessment certificate. The official page is [IQAS assessment for employment, education or licensure](https://www.alberta.ca/iqas-employment-education-licensure) on Alberta.ca.

An IQAS assessment can be used for:

- employment,
- further education, and
- licensure (as supporting information for a regulatory body).

## What IQAS is not

- **It is not an immigration document.** Alberta.ca states that an assessment for employment, education or licensure cannot be used for immigration purposes. Skilled-worker immigration programs require a separate Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) from an organization designated by the federal government. Check IRCC's current list before paying for any assessment.
- **It is not a licence to practise.** Regulated occupations, such as nursing, engineering, teaching and many trades, are controlled by regulatory bodies. They decide whether you may use the title or work in the field, and they may ask for more steps such as exams, language tests or supervised work.
- **It is not a guaranteed result.** Alberta.ca says there is no guarantee that you will be issued an assessment certificate, or that a particular credential will receive the outcome you want.

## Regulated and non-regulated jobs

| Situation | What usually applies |
|---|---|
| A regulated profession (health, engineering, law, teaching, many trades) | Contact the regulatory body first. It sets the steps and may have its own assessment. |
| A non-regulated job (many office, tech, business roles) | The employer decides. An IQAS certificate can help them understand your education. |
| Further study | The school decides which credentials it accepts. Ask admissions what they require. |
| Immigration (Express Entry and similar) | Use an ECA from a designated organization, not IQAS alone. |

## Before you apply

1. **Decide the purpose.** Immigration, work, school or licensing each have different rules.
2. **Ask who will read the result.** Your employer, school or regulatory body may name the service they accept.
3. **Gather documents.** Typically, official transcripts and degree certificates, sent as the service instructs. Read the current document rules on the official page.
4. **Check fees and timelines on the official site.** Third-party websites copy outdated numbers.

## Put it on your resume

If you have an assessment, say so briefly in the education section, for example: "Bachelor of Science, University of X (Country). Assessed by IQAS, Alberta." Then follow standard [Canadian resume practice](/blog/canadian-resume-format-newcomers/).

## Avoid paid middlemen and scams

You can apply to IQAS directly through the government. Be cautious of anyone who promises a guaranteed outcome, asks for payment by gift card or cryptocurrency, or claims to be a "fast-track" agent. Read [how to spot job and recruitment scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is IQAS the same as an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)?

No. Alberta.ca says an IQAS assessment for employment, education or licensure cannot be used for immigration. Immigration programs require an ECA from a designated organization.

### Do I need IQAS to work in Alberta?

Not always. Non-regulated jobs may not require it. Regulated professions need approval from their regulatory body, which may have its own process.

### Will IQAS guarantee that employers accept my degree?

No. It gives employers a clear comparison to Canadian standards, but employers decide.

### How long does an IQAS assessment take, and what does it cost?

Fees and timelines change. Check the official Alberta.ca page rather than a third-party site.

### Can I apply from outside Canada?

IQAS serves people inside and outside Canada, but check the current application instructions on Alberta.ca for how to submit documents.

### Where can I get help choosing the right steps?

Ask the regulatory body for your profession, a settlement agency, or Alberta's career services. Free settlement services exist for newcomers.
