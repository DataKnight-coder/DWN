# Mercor Review and Guide: How It Works, How to Apply and What to Watch For

Mercor is an online marketplace that connects experts with AI companies for contract work such as reviewing and improving AI answers. People often search "is Mercor legit" before applying. This guide uses Mercor's own documentation wherever possible, and clearly marks what comes from third parties.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| What it is | A contractor marketplace that matches experts with AI projects |
| How you apply | Find a role, apply, take an AI interview, receive an offer, then do project work |
| Type of work | Independent contractor, project-based |
| How you are paid | Described in Mercor's docs: hours are logged weekly and paid through Stripe, with Wise as a fallback where Stripe is unavailable |
| Official help | [Mercor talent documentation](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/support/glossary) |

## How to apply, step by step

1. Create a profile at the official Mercor site and complete your details accurately.
2. Browse open roles and apply to the ones that fit your real experience.
3. Take the AI interview. Mercor's glossary says assessments can include AI interviews, multiple-choice quizzes or written submissions.
4. If you receive an offer, read the contract before you accept. Mercor publishes how contracts work on its [contracts page](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/policies/contracts).
5. Follow the [project onboarding guide](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/working/project-onboarding) to set up payments and time tracking.

Screening is mostly the AI interview, quizzes or written tasks, plus a review of your profile. Be accurate about your skills and keep answers specific. ## Pay and payment

Mercor's contract documentation says payments are calculated from hours logged during a weekly cycle and processed the following week, using Stripe with Wise as a fallback where Stripe is not supported. Hourly rates differ by project and are set in each offer, so read the rate before you accept. Third-party sites quote many pay figures, and these are not reliable. Contractors are responsible for their own taxes.

## Things to keep in mind

- Work authorization may still apply for remote roles, so check the terms for your country.
- Only trust messages from official Mercor addresses. Before you upload identity documents, read Mercor's privacy terms and current news about the company, and decide for yourself how much personal data to share.
- Available work changes over time. No guide can promise tasks, income or acceptance.

## Safety checks before you share anything

- A real platform never charges you to apply, test, train or get paid. If anyone asks for money, stop.
- Start only from the platform's official website. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a message.
- Be careful with unsolicited messages on WhatsApp, Telegram or social media, even if they use a real company's name.
- Only upload identity documents inside the official platform, and never send them by chat or email to a "recruiter".
- Read [how to spot AI training and remote job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/) before you apply, and follow the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's guidance if something feels wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Mercor legit?

I cannot vouch for any company. Use the official pages above, read independent experiences, never pay to join, and judge the platform by whether its terms are clear and its payments are documented.

### Is it free to apply?

Yes. A legitimate platform does not charge applicants. If you are asked to pay, treat it as a scam.

### Is the work guaranteed once I am accepted?

No. Projects open and close, and being accepted does not promise a steady supply of tasks. Plan for uneven work.

### Do I need to pay tax on this income?

In Canada, income from contract work is generally taxable and you may need to report it yourself. Ask the Canada Revenue Agency or a tax professional about your situation.

### Can I do Mercor work alongside another job?

Often yes, because the work is flexible and project based. Check each project's time expectations first.

**Educational note:** This guide is general information, not an endorsement of any company, and cannot promise acceptance or income. Platforms change their rules often, so confirm details on their official sites. Results vary.
