# Best Affiliate Programs in Canada: How the Main Options Compare

Choosing an affiliate program is not about the biggest commission number on a list. It is about whether the products suit your audience, whether you can qualify, and whether you understand the terms. This guide compares the main program types available to Canadian publishers and shows what to check on each official page. It does not list commission rates, because rates and rules change and differ by product, category and location; use the linked official pages for current terms. Nothing here guarantees earnings.

New to the topic? Start with our [beginner guide to affiliate marketing in Canada](/blog/affiliate-marketing-canada/).

## How this comparison works

We describe how each program works in general, based on its own published help pages, and tell you what to verify before you join. We have no commercial relationship with these programs. Last reviewed: 19 November 2026. Terms change, so treat the official links below as the source of truth.

## Amazon Associates (Canada)

**What it is:** A direct program that pays commission when customers buy qualifying items through your link on Amazon.ca.

**Worth knowing:** Amazon's Canadian Associates pages describe an application review that depends on qualifying sales in the first period after you join, and a commission is credited only after the customer completes and pays for the order. Commission rates vary by product category. Read the [Associates Program Policies](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/operating/policies) before you apply, including the rules for disclosure and for where your links may appear.

**Best suited to:** Publishers whose audience already shops for physical products and who can recommend items honestly.

## Shopify Affiliate Program

**What it is:** A program for people who introduce new merchants to Shopify. Shopify's help pages explain it is free to join and pays based on qualifying referrals, with amounts that depend on the plan and the merchant's location.

**Worth knowing:** The program is hosted on the impact.com platform, so you manage tracking and payments there. Read [Shopify's affiliate earnings page](https://help.shopify.com/en/affiliates/earnings) for current eligibility and timing.

**Best suited to:** Publishers whose audience is starting or growing an online store, for example readers of a guide on [selling digital products](/blog/how-to-sell-digital-products/).

## Affiliate networks: one account, many advertisers

A network lets you apply to many advertisers from one place. Each advertiser still sets its own terms and approves or rejects you.

### Awin

A global network. Awin's published application information describes a small, refundable joining deposit and a minimum amount you must earn before payout; check the current figures for Canadian dollars on the [Awin application and joining fee page](https://www.awin.com/gb/compliance-and-regulations/application-process-and-joining-fee).

### impact.com

A platform where partners sign up, apply to individual programs and accept each program's contract. Commission terms are set per program. See impact.com's [guide to signing up as a partner](https://help.impact.com/partner/getting-started/sign-up-as-a-partner-on-impactcom).

### Rakuten Advertising

A network where you first join as a publisher and then apply to each advertiser. Some Canadian retailers publish their affiliate information, for example [Walmart Canada](https://www.walmart.ca/en/cp/affiliate-program/6000208941216). Read the [publisher membership agreement](https://rakutenadvertising.com/legal-notices/publisher-membership-agreement/) before you apply.

**Best suited to:** Publishers who want to compare several relevant retailers or services and are willing to apply to each advertiser separately.

## Questions to ask before you join any program

1. **Relevance:** Would my audience genuinely want this product?
2. **Eligibility:** What traffic, content or location requirements apply to me?
3. **What counts:** Which actions earn a commission, and when is it confirmed or reversed?
4. **Payment:** What is the minimum payout, how often is it paid, and in which currency?
5. **Rules:** Where may I place links, and what must I disclose?
6. **Tax:** How will I record and report the income in Canada?

## Disclose and keep records

The [Competition Bureau says material connections, including commissions, should be disclosed clearly and prominently](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act). Commission income may also be taxable; see the [CRA's platform-economy guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/platform-economy/understanding-tax-obligations.html). If you hold a work permit, check that earning this income is allowed under your conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best affiliate program in Canada?

There is no single best one. The best program is relevant to your audience, accepts you, and has terms you understand. Compare official program pages rather than headline commission rates.

### Do I need a website to join?

Requirements vary by program and advertiser. Some ask for a website, social channel or other content so they can review your audience. Check each program's eligibility page.

### Is affiliate income taxable in Canada?

Commission income is generally reportable. Keep records and check the CRA's guidance or ask a qualified tax professional.

### Do these programs charge fees?

Some programs are free to join and others charge a small refundable deposit. Confirm current costs on the official page before you apply.
