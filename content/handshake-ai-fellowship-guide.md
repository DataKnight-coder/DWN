# Handshake AI Guide: What the Fellowship Is and How to Apply

Handshake AI is the AI-training arm of Handshake, the career platform known for connecting students and employers. It places domain experts on projects that evaluate and improve AI models. This guide uses Handshake's help-centre pages and marks third-party claims clearly.

## How to apply, step by step

1. Go to the official Handshake AI page and read who is eligible in your country.
2. Apply with an accurate profile that shows your education and subject expertise.
3. Complete any assessment or interview requested inside the platform.
4. Read the Project Terms for each project before you accept. They set the rate, whether it is hourly or per task, and whether onboarding is paid.
5. Track your hours and payments, and keep your own records.

## What the screening checks

Handshake looks at your education, expertise and any task-based assessments. Domain depth matters more than general enthusiasm. The guide to [preparing for an AI training interview](/blog/ai-interview-assessment-prep/) explains the common formats.

## Pay and payment

Handshake's help centre says most projects pay either an hourly rate or a per-task rate, as outlined in the Project Terms, and onboarding is paid only if the Project Terms say so. Third-party sites quote wide ranges, but only the Project Terms for your project count. Work is not guaranteed week to week.

## Things to keep in mind

- Handshake's help pages describe a referral programme for some users. Do not pay for or buy referrals.
- Check whether you will be classed as an independent contractor and what that means for tax.
- Available work changes over time. No guide can promise tasks, income or acceptance.

## Who Handshake AI may suit

Students, graduates and professionals with strong subject expertise, particularly in specialist fields. Compare other options in [AI training platforms compared](/blog/ai-training-platforms-compared/) and read the full [AI training jobs guide](/blog/ai-training-jobs-guide/).

## Safety checks before you share anything

- A real platform never charges you to apply, test, train or get paid. If anyone asks for money, stop.
- Start only from the platform's official website. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a message.
- Be careful with unsolicited messages on WhatsApp, Telegram or social media, even if they use a real company's name.
- Only upload identity documents inside the official platform, and never send them by chat or email to a "recruiter".
- Read [how to spot AI training and remote job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/) before you apply, and follow the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's guidance if something feels wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Handshake AI legit?

I cannot vouch for any company. Use the official pages above, read independent experiences, never pay to join, and judge the platform by whether its terms are clear and its payments are documented.

### Is it free to apply?

Yes. A legitimate platform does not charge applicants. If you are asked to pay, treat it as a scam.

### Is the work guaranteed once I am accepted?

No. Projects open and close, and being accepted does not promise a steady supply of tasks. Plan for uneven work.

### Do I need to pay tax on this income?

In Canada, income from contract work is generally taxable and you may need to report it yourself. Ask the Canada Revenue Agency or a tax professional about your situation.

### Can I do Handshake AI work alongside another job?

Often yes, because the work is flexible and project based. Check each project's time expectations first.

**Educational note:** This guide is general information, not an endorsement of any company, and cannot promise acceptance or income. Platforms change their rules often, so confirm details on their official sites. Results vary.
