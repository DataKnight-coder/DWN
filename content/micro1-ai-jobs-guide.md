Pay depends on the project and is shown in the offer. Third-party reviews quote many figures, so rely on the details in your own offer. Work is matched to project demand and is not guaranteed.

## Things to keep in mind

- Test your camera, microphone and internet before starting, because candidate reports mention timers running during questions.
- Your interview recording may be stored, so read the privacy terms before you begin.
- Available work changes over time. No guide can promise tasks, income or acceptance.

## Safety checks before you share anything

- A real platform never charges you to apply, test, train or get paid. If anyone asks for money, stop.
- Start only from the platform's official website. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a message.
- Be careful with unsolicited messages on WhatsApp, Telegram or social media, even if they use a real company's name.
- Only upload identity documents inside the official platform, and never send them by chat or email to a "recruiter".
- Read [how to spot AI training and remote job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/) before you apply, and follow the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's guidance if something feels wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is micro1 legit?

I cannot vouch for any company. Use the official pages above, read independent experiences, never pay to join, and judge the platform by whether its terms are clear and its payments are documented.

### Is it free to apply?

Yes. A legitimate platform does not charge applicants. If you are asked to pay, treat it as a scam.

### Is the work guaranteed once I am accepted?

No. Projects open and close, and being accepted does not promise a steady supply of tasks. Plan for uneven work.

### Do I need to pay tax on this income?

In Canada, income from contract work is generally taxable and you may need to report it yourself. Ask the Canada Revenue Agency or a tax professional about your situation.

### Can I do micro1 work alongside another job?

Often yes, because the work is flexible and project based. Check each project's time expectations first.
