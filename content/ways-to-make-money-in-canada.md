# 37 Ways to Make Money in Canada: Legitimate Ideas for Newcomers and Professionals

There is no single best way to earn extra income in Canada. The right choice depends on your skills, your time, your money and your immigration status. This list gives 37 legitimate ideas in four groups, so you can pick two or three to test. No income is guaranteed for any of them, and results vary a lot from person to person.

## Before you start: three quick checks

1. **Your status.** Some permits limit work or self-employment. Confirm what yours allows with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or a regulated immigration professional.
2. **Tax.** The Canada Revenue Agency says self-employment income, including online and gig income, must be reported. See the CRA's page on the [platform and gig economy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/platform-economy/gig-economy.html).
3. **Scams.** Never pay to get a job, and be careful with anyone who promises fast or guaranteed money. Read [how to spot job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/).

## A. Ways to earn online

1. **Freelance writing or editing.** Offer articles, copy or proofreading to businesses.
2. **Virtual assistance.** Handle email, scheduling and admin for busy owners.
3. **Social media management.** Run posting and replies for small local businesses.
4. **Online tutoring.** Teach a school subject, a language or a professional skill.
5. **Translation.** Offer translation if you are fluent in two languages.
6. **Graphic or web design.** Make logos, slides or simple websites.
7. **Bookkeeping help.** Assist small businesses with records, where the rules allow.
8. **UGC content creation.** Make short videos or photos for brands. See [how to become a UGC creator](/blog/ugc-creator-guide/).
9. **Selling digital products.** Sell templates, guides or checklists. See [how to sell digital products](/blog/how-to-sell-digital-products/).
10. **Affiliate marketing.** Recommend products and earn a commission, with disclosure. See [affiliate marketing in Canada](/blog/affiliate-marketing-canada/).

## B. Ways to use your professional skills

11. **Consulting.** Advise a small business in your field of expertise.
12. **Coaching or mentoring.** Share what you know, if your profession's rules allow it.
13. **Teaching a short course.** Run a workshop online or at a community centre.
14. **Public speaking.** Speak at events about your field.
15. **Resume and interview help.** Help job seekers present themselves well.
16. **Contract or part-time work in your field.** Many companies hire contractors for projects.
17. **Technical support.** Help individuals or small firms with computers and software.
18. **Research or data work.** Offer research, analysis or spreadsheet help.
19. **Remote jobs.** Look for genuine remote roles. See [how to find legitimate remote jobs](/blog/remote-jobs-guide/).
20. **AI-training and evaluation work.** Project-based work that improves AI systems. See [AI training jobs](/blog/ai-training-jobs-guide/), and check each company's terms first.

## C. Local and gig ideas

21. **Delivery or rideshare driving.** Check licence, insurance and work-eligibility rules.
22. **Snow removal.** A practical seasonal service in many Canadian cities.
23. **Lawn care and yard work.** A summer service for homes.
24. **House cleaning.** Many households and small offices pay for regular cleaning.
25. **Handyman or moving help.** Small repairs, assembly and moving help.
26. **Pet sitting or dog walking.** Look after pets while owners are away.
27. **Child care or after-school help.** Check licensing and background-check rules before you start.
28. **Catering or baking.** Check food-safety rules and local licences first.
29. **Event help.** Part-time work at events, weddings and festivals.

## D. Ways to earn from things you own or sell

30. **Selling unused items.** Sell what you no longer need on online marketplaces.
31. **Reselling second-hand goods.** Buy low and sell higher, with honest descriptions.
32. **Selling handmade items.** Craft, bake or make products and sell them online.
33. **Print on demand.** Sell designs on products that a partner prints and ships.
34. **Dropshipping, with the rules.** Learn each platform's rules before you start. See [dropshipping for beginners](/blog/dropshipping-beginners-guide/).
35. **Renting a room or space.** Check your lease, local bylaws and insurance first.
36. **Renting storage or parking space.** Only if your lease and local rules allow it.
37. **Short-term rental.** Many cities have strict rules, so check them before you list anything.

## How to choose two or three to test

- Start with the skill you already have.
- Choose something you can test with little money.
- Talk to ten potential customers before you build anything.
- Keep records of every dollar from day one.

## What most people get wrong

- Trying five ideas at once.
- Spending money before getting a paying customer.
- Ignoring tax and licence rules until later.
- Believing income screenshots and promises.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the easiest way to make money in Canada?

There is no single easiest way. The simplest start is usually something that uses a skill you already have and needs little money, such as tutoring, a local service or freelance work for people you know.

### Can newcomers do these jobs?

Many can, but it depends on your immigration status and permit. Confirm with IRCC or a regulated immigration professional before you start.

### Do I have to pay tax on side income?

Generally yes. The CRA says self-employment income must be reported. Keep records and check the current rules.

### Which ideas need a licence?

Food, child care, driving for hire, short-term rental and some regulated professions can need licences or permits. Check your city and province.

### How much can I earn?

It varies widely and nobody can promise an amount. Be careful with anyone who does.
