# Outlier AI Guide: How to Join, What the Work Is and How Pay Is Described

Outlier is a platform where contributors help train and evaluate AI models. It describes itself as powered by Scale AI. Many people searching "Outlier AI" ask whether it is real, how to start and why some contributors wait for tasks. This guide uses Outlier's own pages where possible.

## How to apply, step by step

1. Create an account on Outlier's official website.
2. Choose your areas of expertise and verify your identity inside the platform.
3. Complete the short skill screenings. Outlier uses them to match you with projects.
4. If there is no project for you yet, Outlier says qualified people may stay in its contributor pool and be contacted when projects open.
5. Use the Help Center inside your dashboard for support. Outlier's pages say to use the "Need Help?" icon.

## What the screening checks

Outlier uses short skill assessments rather than a long interview. Read each instruction carefully, work in your own words and follow the style rules for the project. The guide to [preparing for an AI training interview](/blog/ai-interview-assessment-prep/) explains the common formats.

## Pay and payment

Outlier says the exact pay for a project is clearly stated before you begin, and its own pages list rates that vary by skill and language. Third-party reviews quote very different numbers, so treat them as opinions. A common complaint is passing an assessment but waiting for tasks. Task supply varies, so do not treat the platform as a steady job.

## Things to keep in mind

- Contributors in different countries may see different projects.
- Keep records of your hours and payments for tax time.
- Available work changes over time. No guide can promise tasks, income or acceptance.

## Who Outlier may suit

People with strong writing, language, subject-matter or reasoning skills who are comfortable with flexible, uneven work. Compare other options in [AI training platforms compared](/blog/ai-training-platforms-compared/) and read the full [AI training jobs guide](/blog/ai-training-jobs-guide/).

## Safety checks before you share anything

- A real platform never charges you to apply, test, train or get paid. If anyone asks for money, stop.
- Start only from the platform's official website. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a message.
- Be careful with unsolicited messages on WhatsApp, Telegram or social media, even if they use a real company's name.
- Only upload identity documents inside the official platform, and never send them by chat or email to a "recruiter".
- Read [how to spot AI training and remote job scams](/blog/ai-training-job-scams/) before you apply, and follow the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's guidance if something feels wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Outlier legit?

I cannot vouch for any company. Use the official pages above, read independent experiences, never pay to join, and judge the platform by whether its terms are clear and its payments are documented.

### Is it free to apply?

Yes. A legitimate platform does not charge applicants. If you are asked to pay, treat it as a scam.

### Is the work guaranteed once I am accepted?

No. Projects open and close, and being accepted does not promise a steady supply of tasks. Plan for uneven work.

### Do I need to pay tax on this income?

In Canada, income from contract work is generally taxable and you may need to report it yourself. Ask the Canada Revenue Agency or a tax professional about your situation.

### Can I do Outlier work alongside another job?

Often yes, because the work is flexible and project based. Check each project's time expectations first.

**Educational note:** This guide is general information, not an endorsement of any company, and cannot promise acceptance or income. Platforms change their rules often, so confirm details on their official sites. Results vary.
