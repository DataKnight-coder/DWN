## Build order (quality over quantity)

Priority is highest first, based on how many people search the topic, how well it fits Dr. Hauwa's real expertise, and how directly it leads to a lead or membership.

1. **Immigrant career basics (area 21).** Canadian resume format, credential recognition. Large audience, fits the brand, no income claims.
2. **AI interview and assessment prep (area 2).** Follows from the strongest existing guides.
3. **Financial education for newcomers (area 20).** TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, building credit. Needs official Canada.ca sources.
4. **Platform reviews (area 1).** Mercor, Outlier, micro1, Handshake AI, one guide each, with the scam-check section.
5. **Freelancing and Upwork (area 11).**
6. **First-time buyer for newcomers (area 19).** Strictly educational, reviewed.
7. **Generative AI tools and prompt basics (area 3).**
8. **LinkedIn and personal branding (area 15).**

Each guide follows the same standard: a direct answer in the first lines, a real tutorial, a FAQ drawn from real search questions, official sources linked (Canada.ca, Competition Bureau, CRA, platform help pages), the author block for Dr. Hauwa, and internal links to at least two related guides.

## Not done, on purpose

- No new "membership benefit" claims until the course library is checked.
- No testimonials, case studies, media coverage or credentials added until Dr. Hauwa supplies real ones with permission.
- No Google Preferred Sources button until eligibility is confirmed from Google's current documentation.
- Competitor analysis and KPIs wait for Search Console access.
