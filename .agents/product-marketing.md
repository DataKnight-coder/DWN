# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Diaspora Wealth Network connects immigrants and diaspora professionals interested in career, business, and long-term asset development.
**Business model:** Paid membership via Selfany; consultation enquiries via the website. Pricing and terms are controlled by Selfany and must be verified there.

## Target Audience

Immigrants, diaspora professionals, and entrepreneurs exploring career development, digital opportunities, business, and asset building. The site serves visitors at different stages, so the free webinar is a lower-commitment path and membership is a separate paid decision.

## Problems & Pain Points

The founder's approved article describes the gap between earning income and building assets, difficulty translating skills into opportunities, and the need for a professional network in a new country.

## Differentiation
