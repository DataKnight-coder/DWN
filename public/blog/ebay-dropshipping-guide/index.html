People often ask whether you can dropship on eBay, and especially whether you can list an item on eBay and fulfil it from Amazon. The short answer: dropshipping on eBay is allowed when your supplier ships directly to your customer, but eBay does not allow you to list an item, sell it and then buy it from another retailer or marketplace such as Amazon or Walmart to ship straight to the buyer. This guide explains the difference, what a safe setup looks like and what to check. It is general information, and eBay's current policy pages are the source of truth.

The rule in plain language

  • Allowed: you sell an item you do not hold, and a wholesale supplier or manufacturer with whom you have an arrangement ships it directly to your customer.
  • Not allowed: you list an item on eBay, a customer buys it, and you then purchase it from another retailer or marketplace, such as Amazon or Walmart, which ships it to your customer.
  • Allowed: holding your own stock in a fulfilment warehouse, including a marketplace's warehouse program, and having it ship your eBay orders.

A common tell that a seller broke the rule is a gift receipt or another retailer's packaging in the parcel, because the item was bought only after it sold. Buyers notice, and it can lead to complaints and account restrictions.

Policies are updated over time, and community discussions often paraphrase them imperfectly. Always read the current policy in eBay's Help section (search for "dropshipping") on your own country's eBay site before you list.

What a compliant setup looks like

  1. Get a real supplier. Use a wholesaler, distributor or manufacturer that agrees to ship individual orders to your customers under your name, ideally in writing.
  2. List only what you can fulfil. Confirm stock before you list, and keep listings synchronised so you do not sell items that are out of stock.
  3. State honest handling and delivery times. Dropshipped items can take longer than local stock, and eBay buyers expect the times you promise.
  4. Describe the item accurately and use photos you have the right to use.
  5. Plan returns. The buyer returns to you, so know what your supplier accepts and who pays shipping.
  6. Track costs per order: item cost, eBay fees, shipping and payment fees, returns and your time.
  7. Keep records for tax. See the CRA's guidance for people who earn through digital platforms, or your own country's authority.

Common mistakes

  • Sourcing from big retailers after the sale. This is the main policy problem described above.
  • Overpromising delivery times. It is the most common cause of negative feedback.
  • Ignoring returns and disputes. Plan for them before they happen.
  • Copying other sellers' listings and images. It can breach copyright and eBay's rules.
  • Scaling before testing. Start with a few products and learn from real orders.

Where eBay fits among your options

eBay gives you access to existing buyers, but you compete on price and reputation. If you want more control over branding and the customer relationship, compare it with your own Shopify store and Amazon. Amazon has its own, stricter rules; see Amazon dropshipping rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is dropshipping allowed on eBay?

Yes, if your supplier ships the item directly to your customer. It is not allowed to list an item and then buy it from another retailer or marketplace, such as Amazon or Walmart, to ship to your buyer.

Can I list Amazon products on eBay and ship them directly to buyers?

No. That is the practice eBay does not allow, and Amazon also prohibits it. Use a genuine wholesale supplier or your own stock instead.

Can I use my own stock stored in a fulfilment warehouse?

Yes. Holding your own inventory in a fulfilment warehouse and having it ship your eBay orders is a different model from buying elsewhere after the sale.

What happens if I break the policy?

You can face listing removals, selling limits or account restrictions. Read the current policy and keep a written agreement with your supplier.

Do I need a business licence to sell on eBay?

It depends on where you live and how much you sell. Ask your local authority or a qualified professional. In Canada, check the CRA's rules for GST/HST registration and reporting income.

How much can I make dropshipping on eBay?

No amount is guaranteed. Margins are often thin after fees, shipping and returns. Track every cost per order before you scale.
