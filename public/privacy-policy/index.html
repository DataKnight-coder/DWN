Website information

Privacy and your website enquiries.

This page explains what the Dr. Hauwa website collects through its own forms and tracking choices. External providers have their own practices.

Information you choose to provide

Our forms may collect your name, email address, interest, optional country and message. We save the form source, page, time, referral or campaign parameters and your email marketing choice in the website CRM. We use that information to review your enquiry, manage follow-up and understand which website resources are useful.

Email choices

The marketing checkbox is optional on the free guide form. The website currently has no connected automated email sender. We record explicit opt-in, and you can use the unsubscribe form or contact us through the website to change your preference.

Website measurement

If you allow optional website tracking, the site records anonymous popup impressions, successful popup submissions and clicks to external offers. We do not treat those clicks as purchases. If advertising measurement is switched on for this website, Meta's Pixel may receive page views, form submissions and offer clicks, but only after you choose Allow tracking. It does not receive your name or email address, and you can change your choice at any time through Tracking choices. No automated marketing emails are sent without your separate consent. Cloudflare may collect basic operational and visit data separately.

External services

Selfany handles its own guide access, enrollment, bookings and payments. Stan Store hosts the housing blueprint. Their privacy terms apply when you visit those services. Cloudflare provides the website hosting, database, security checks and storage.

Questions

Use the website enquiry form for questions about information submitted here. We do not publish a fixed retention period because one has not been established in this project.
