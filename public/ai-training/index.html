Who this is for

Professionals who want to work more efficiently, entrepreneurs testing helpful workflows, and immigrants exploring digital opportunities with realistic expectations.

Learning focus

Practical use of AI for research, content planning, administrative workflows, customer questions and business problem solving. We encourage checking outputs, respecting privacy and applying professional judgement.

Topics to explore

Clear instructions and review

Frame a useful task, provide appropriate context, evaluate the answer and correct errors before using it.

Productivity and digital work

Identify repetitive tasks that can be supported by AI, while keeping sensitive information secure.

Business workflows

Map a customer need and test a small, measurable workflow before scaling it.
