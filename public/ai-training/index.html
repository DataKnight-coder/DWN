Who it is for

Professionals, students, career changers, entrepreneurs and people building extra income. You do not need to be technical to begin. Whether you are an immigrant, a newcomer or a long-time resident, the goal is the same: realistic skills and safe choices.

What AI-training work involves

Ranking and rating answers

Compare responses, decide which is better and explain why. This human feedback helps models improve.

Writing prompts and ideal answers

Create questions and model responses in your subject area or language.

Checking facts and reasoning

Verify accuracy, spot errors and apply professional judgment in fields such as finance, law, health, science, teaching or languages.

Reviewing and correcting code

Fix and explain auto-generated scripts for people with programming skills.

Safety testing

Probe where a model fails or behaves badly, following strict project guidelines.

Platforms people ask about

Each platform describes itself differently and sets its own eligibility, screening and pay. Read our side-by-side comparison and each company's official pages before applying. Naming a platform here is not an endorsement, and DWN is not affiliated with any of them.

Mercor

A talent marketplace connecting domain experts with AI labs and enterprises. Applicants typically complete an AI-led interview in a chosen domain.

micro1

A data company that supplies experts for AI training and evaluation and screens applicants with an AI interviewer. Roles are independent contractor work.

Handshake AI

Handshake's program for students, graduates and professionals doing paid, part-time, project-based AI evaluation work. Eligibility conditions apply, so check your country and status.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI training work?

It is paid work that helps improve AI systems: ranking and rating answers, checking facts, writing prompts and ideal responses, reviewing code and testing for safety. Companies use human judgment to make models more accurate and useful.

Is AI training work a real job?

Real work exists, usually as part-time or project-based contracting rather than full-time employment. Hours are often not guaranteed. Scammers also copy this kind of work, so read our guide to spotting job scams before you apply.

What are Mercor, micro1 and Handshake AI?

They are companies that connect skilled people with AI labs and businesses that need models trained and evaluated. Each sets its own eligibility, screening and pay. See our comparison for what each describes publicly and what to check.

Do I need a degree or technical background?

Not always. Some entry-level projects value careful reading and following instructions. Expert projects ask for proven experience or qualifications in a subject, which can be professional experience as well as a degree.

Can I do AI training work from Canada or outside the United States?

Often yes, but it depends on the platform and project. Some programs limit eligibility by country or work authorization, so read each current eligibility page before you apply.

How much does AI training work pay?

It varies widely by project, expertise and platform, and no amount is guaranteed. Check the rate on the actual listing and treat figures in videos or posts as examples only.

Do I have to pay to apply or to get access to tasks?

No. Legitimate platforms do not charge applicants for access, tests, training or pay. A request for money is a major warning sign.

Can AI training work replace my job?

For most people it is extra, flexible income, not a guaranteed replacement for a stable job. Hours and availability change, so plan around that.
