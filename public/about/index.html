About

I started in a Walmart freezer. With a PhD.

I am Dr. Hauwa Christiana Amiebenomo, PhD. I am a realtor, a cybersecurity analyst and the founder of the Diaspora Wealth Network (DWN). I teach practical skills in plain language, so that nobody has to figure it out the hard way.

My story

I started in Canada working in a Walmart freezer, even though I had a PhD. I thought a job was the whole plan. It was not. I became a cybersecurity analyst, then a realtor, and I kept learning new skills on the side.

What helped most was learning how things really work, step by step. When I wanted to invest in real estate, I had no one to ask, so I invested in a mentor and learned before I bought. You can read that story in how to start real estate investing in Canada with no experience.

My background

  • Education: a PhD in Petroleum Engineering (University of Port Harcourt) and a postgraduate degree in Cyber/Computer Forensics and Counterterrorism (CDI College, Canada).
  • Certifications: Certified Information Security Manager (CISM), CompTIA Security+, CompTIA A+ and ITIL Foundation.
  • Experience: since 2016 I have worked in identity and access management and cybersecurity, in banking, credit union and research environments, in Nigeria and in Canada. I have also worked as a remote AI trainer and prompt reviewer, so I teach AI-training work from the inside.
  • Based in: Alberta, Canada.

What I teach

  • AI-training work. How real opportunities work, and how to stay safe from the fake ones.
  • Online income and selling. Selling online without breaking platform rules, and turning skills into income.
  • Real estate. Homeownership, house hacking and housing costs, as education.
  • Careers and cybersecurity. Changing careers, resumes, LinkedIn and getting into tech.
  • AI video and YouTube. Using AI tools to edit and animate video, and what YouTube allows.
  • Credit and money basics. How credit works and how to build it as a newcomer.
  • Personal branding and entrepreneurship. Being clear about what you offer, and starting a business.

How I work

No hype. Nobody can promise you results, and I will not. I teach what I have learned and what official sources say, and I tell you when the rules differ by country or city. Read the full disclaimer.
